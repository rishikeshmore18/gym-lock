## Overview

**How to read this doc:** a bolded **Change** line under any diagram or table row means the app doesn't work that way today and this is the change being proposed. Anything without one is already built and working.

### Who we're building for (from the research)

- **They want to go more than they actually do.** Onboarding asks for both numbers.
- **Most go in the evening.** 6pm is the busiest gym hour.
- **Half of new gym members drop off by week 6.**
- **What breaks them isn't one missed day.** It's a bad week that turns into "forget it."
- **Late-night scrolling eats their sleep.** In a small trial, no phone before bed meant falling asleep faster and sleeping longer.

So the design is **strict in the moment** (apps lock), **forgiving across the week** (3 days keeps the streak, reschedules, freezes), and **honest about proof** (GPS proves you showed up; Apple Health, 20 minutes at the gym, or a photo proves you trained).

## Step 0 — Which Flow the User Gets

```text
 gap = gym time − wake time

   gap ≤ 2 hours  ──►  WAKE & GO   (the wake-up alarm is the gym alarm)
   gap > 2 hours  ──►  GO LATER    (a separate "time to go" alarm)

 BOTH get the night lock, every night.
```

**Change:** today the app decides by the clock — an alarm before 11:00 counts as "morning." Evening users also get their wake time set to their gym alarm (e.g. 17:15).

## The Night Lock (Everyone, Every Sleep Night)

```text
 BEDTIME  23:00 ──► distracting apps LOCKED  (automatic, no switch)
                      calls, texts, maps and alarms still work
                      │
 WAKE     06:30 ──► night lock ends
                      ├─ gym day (wake & go) ──► gym lock takes over
                      │                           straight away, no gap
                      └─ any other day        ──► apps unlocked
```

### Which nights (the day card on the Alarm screen)

```text
 tap the sleep bar ──► "sleep schedule" · every night by default
 tap the gym bar   ──► "gym days" · e.g. Mon · Wed · Fri · 3 minimum

 the night before a gym day is always on
   try to turn it off ──► short note (disappears after ~3 s):
   "monday is a gym day. keep sunday night on for rest."

 a night belongs to the day it starts: "Sun" = Sunday 23:00 → Monday 06:30
```

This is already built, except:

- **Built (`2026-09-26`):** today the gym days card can go down to 0 days. Minimum 3, with a note: "3 gym days is the minimum."
- **Change:** today nothing is protected when bedtime is after midnight (e.g. 00:30). The night that ends on a gym morning must always stay on.

### Gym and sleep never overlap (on the dial)

```text
 drag the gym bar INTO the night
   ──► the night slides later and keeps its length,
       with travel time home in between (at least 30 min)

   before:  sleep 23:00 → 07:00   +   gym 23:00 → 00:00    ✗ overlap
   after:   gym 23:00 → 00:00  ·  travel  ·  sleep 00:30 → 08:30
   note:    "moved your sleep to 00:30–08:30 so it starts after the gym."

 drag the night INTO the gym ──► it stops at the gym (as today)
```

**Change:** today the gym bar stops 30 min before bedtime. It never pushes the night.

### Goes to the gym in sleep hours anyway

```text
 plan: gym 17:00 · sleep 23:00 → 07:00 · actually goes at 23:00
   ──► night lock STAYS ON (calls and maps still work)
   ──► workout done (Flow 3) ──► +1 day
   ──► 🔔 "workout counted. it cost you sleep though.
          aim to be home by 23:00."
```

**Change:** new. Today a gym visit with no alarm running isn't noticed or counted.

- **Change:** today the night lock has an on/off switch, and it starts on only if the user said in onboarding that they scroll at night. Remove the switch: it always runs on the sleep schedule's nights.
- **Change:** today only those users are asked for a bedtime. Ask everyone for a bedtime and a wake time.
- **Change:** today it only locks when the user next opens the app after bedtime, backed by a reminder notification. Add the background Screen Time extension (a DeviceActivityMonitor extension) so it locks at 23:00 even if the app is never opened.
- **Change:** remove the "custom window" option. The lock always runs from bedtime to wake time.

### Edge cases

| What happens | What the app does |
| --- | --- |
| Needs the phone at 1am (a taxi home, a family emergency) | Only the chosen distracting apps are locked. Calls, messages, maps and alarms all work. |
| Moves bedtime to 3am to dodge the lock tonight | A new bedtime starts from **tomorrow night**. *(Change)* |
| Sets a 5-minute "sleep" to dodge it | The sleep schedule must be at least 5 hours. *(Change)* |
| Works nights and sleeps 09:00 to 16:00 | The lock follows their own sleep hours, whatever they are. |
| Turns off a night that isn't before a gym day | Allowed. No night lock that night. Already built. |
| Wakes at 5:00 on a rest day | The lock stays until wake time. No button unlocks it. |
| Hasn't picked any apps to block | Nothing to lock. The app says so and asks them to pick apps. |

## Flow 1 — Wake & Go

*Plan: Mon/Wed/Fri. Wake 6:30, 20 min to get ready, 15 min to travel, so gym by 7:05.*

```text
EVERY NIGHT (sleep schedule)
 23:00  night lock ON ──► ends 06:30

GYM DAY (Mon / Wed / Fri)
 06:30  ⏰ alarm rings ──► apps LOCKED  (night lock hands over, no gap)
          │
          ├─ [5 more min]  (only once)
          │     └─ 06:35 rings again. apps still locked. no more snooze.
          │
          ├─ [I'm up]
          │     └─ quick task (e.g. 20 steps)  ← if "persistent mode" is on
          │          └─ countdown: "gym by 7:05"
          │               └─ phone notices you left home
          │                    └─ arrive (2 min inside the gym area)
          │                         └─ ✅ apps UNLOCKED
          │                              └─ AT THE GYM (Flow 3)
          │                                   workout done ──► +1 day
          │
          ├─ [can't today] ──► SKIP FLOW (Flow 4)
          │
          └─ (ignores everything / falls back asleep)
                └─ apps stay locked until 08:35 (gym time + 90 min, max 4 h)
                     └─ lock lifts
                          └─ 🔔 "missed this morning. pick a day to make it up."
                               └─ SKIP FLOW (same options as "can't today")

REST DAY (Tue / Thu / Sat / Sun)
 06:30  night lock ends ──► apps unlocked
 06:30  ⏰ plain alarm, same time, NO lock (optional)
```

**Change:** today an ignored alarm never ends. Nothing is recorded, no reschedule is offered, and if the app stays open in the background the next day's alarm can't start.

**Change:** the rest-day plain alarm doesn't exist yet.

### Edge cases

| What happens | What the app does |
| --- | --- |
| Woke up before the alarm | "Start early" button. The gym lock takes over from the night lock. Already built. |
| Countdown runs out on the way | "Still going" gives a few extra minutes. Already built. |
| At the gym but GPS can't confirm | "I'm here" button, apps unlock. Already built. How the day counts: see Flow 3. |
| Snoozed, then slept through the second ring | Same as ignoring: locked until 08:35, then the reschedule notification. |
| Phone was off or dead, alarm never rang | Nothing is recorded and there's no penalty screen. The next time the app opens, it offers a reschedule. *(Change)* |

## Flow 2 — Go Later (Midday, Evening, Night)

*Plan: Mon/Wed/Fri. Wake 7:00, gym at 18:00, 10 min to get ready, 20 min to travel, so the "time to go" alarm is at 17:30.*

```text
EVERY NIGHT     23:00  night lock ON ──► ends 07:00
EVERY MORNING   07:00  plain wake alarm (optional, NO lock)
DAYTIME                nothing locked

GYM DAY
 17:30  ⏰ "time to go" alarm ──► apps LOCKED
          │
          ├─ [I'm going] ──► countdown "gym by 18:00" ──► leave ──► arrive
          │                    └─ ✅ UNLOCKED ──► AT THE GYM (Flow 3)
          │
          ├─ [running late]  (only once, no snooze here, they're awake)
          │     └─ pick +15 / +30 / +60 ──► rings again then, apps STAY locked
          │        (options that would run into sleep hours are hidden)
          │
          ├─ [can't today] ──► SKIP FLOW (Flow 4)
          │
          └─ (ignores it) ──► locked until 19:30 ──► lifts
                               └─ 🔔 "missed tonight. pick a day to make it up."
                                    └─ SKIP FLOW
```

**Change:** the "time to go" alarm is new — today the gym bar at 18:00 on the dial does nothing.

**Change:** today "move time" UNLOCKS the apps and only sends a normal notification. It's a free unlock. "Running late" should keep the lock and set a real alarm instead. Options that run into sleep hours are hidden.

### Edge cases

| What happens | What the app does |
| --- | --- |
| Stuck at work at 17:30 | "Running late," once. |
| Running late would push the gym into sleep hours | Those options are hidden. *(Change)* |
| Went to the gym on a day that isn't in the plan | It counts once the workout is done (Flow 3). *(Change: today a visit only counts during an alarm session)* |
| Gym at 21:00, done 22:00, bedtime 23:00 | Fits: at least 30 min to get home before bedtime. Already built. |
| Planned gym at 23:30 that runs past midnight | The night already starts after it. Count the visit on the day the alarm rang. *(Change: today it counts on the arrival day, so a Sunday-night visit lands in next week)* |

## Flow 3 — At the Gym (When the Day Counts + Notifications)

- **Arriving (GPS) unlocks the apps.** The lock's job is done.
- **Finishing the workout counts the day.** "Done" means an Apple Health workout of 20+ min, or 20 min at the gym, whichever comes first.

```text
 ARRIVE at the gym (GPS, 2 min inside) ──► ✅ apps UNLOCKED
   │
   └─ 🔔 "you're in. 20 minutes and today counts."
   │
   ▼
 IS THE WORKOUT DONE?  (whichever comes first)
   • Apple Health shows a workout: 20 min or longer, started at the gym
     (at most 15 min before arrival), not typed in by hand
   • OR 20 minutes at the gym  (for people with no watch or no Health data)
   │
   ├─ YES ──► +1 day this week · 🏅 "workout verified" sticker
   │            │
   │            └─ 🔔 at 30 min in the gym OR when they leave, whichever is first:
   │                 • "2 of 3 this week. share your progress."
   │                 • "that's 3. this one keeps your streak."
   │                 • "4 this week. that's a bonus day."
   │                 • "you came back. that's the hardest one."
   │                 (never the same line twice in a row)
   │                 └─ tap ──► PROGRESS SPOTLIGHT (below)
   │
   └─ NO: left before 20 min, no Health workout
        └─ 🔔 "you left after 12 min. 20 minutes makes it count."
             └─ SKIP FLOW (reschedule / home workout / skip)
                (a 20-min Apple Health workout within 5 hours still counts)
```

### The Progress spotlight (opened from that notification)

```text
   ┌────────────────────────────────┐
   │░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│   everything dimmed
   │░░  share your progress  ░░░░░░░│   text sits in the dark area
   │░░ ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓ ░░│
   │░░ ┃   today's photo card   ┃ ░░│   only this card is lit,
   │░░ ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛ ░░│   with a glowing edge
   │░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│
   └────────────────────────────────┘
   tap the card ──► take / share today's photo
   tap anywhere else ──► spotlight closes
   shown once per workout day, only from that notification
```

- **Change:** today the day counts the moment you arrive. Count it when the workout is done.
- **Change:** today any Health workout counts, even 1 minute or typed in by hand. Set a 20-minute minimum and ignore hand-typed ones.
- **Change:** today the app stops watching the gym area once arrival is confirmed. It can't tell how long you stayed or when you left. Track both.
- **Change:** today the only gym notification is "you're here. 🔥 apps unlocked. go train." The workout-done notification and the spotlight are new.
- **Built (`2026-09-26`), bug:** today tapping ANY GymLock notification counts as "I'm up" on an alarm. After a gym visit, that can lock the apps again for up to 2 hours. Only alarm notifications may start a session. The gym notification opens Progress.
- **Change:** today "verified" in the share stickers means a GPS visit. It now means the workout was done. GPS arrivals alone become "gym visits."

### Edge cases

| What happens | What the app does |
| --- | --- |
| No Apple Watch and no workout app | 20 min at the gym counts, sticker included. *(Change)* |
| Apple Health shows 12 min but they stayed 35 min | Counts. Either proof is enough, and neither cancels the other. |
| The watch syncs to the phone an hour later | If they stayed 20 min, it's already counted. If not, Apple Health is checked for 5 hours and it counts when the workout shows up. |
| Workout logged by hand in the Health app | Ignored. |
| Health permission turned off | The app can't tell this apart from "no workout" (Apple hides it). 20 min at the gym still counts. The message points them to the permission. |
| Steps out to the car for 3 min | Short trips outside (under 5 min) don't reset the clock. |
| GPS can't confirm, taps "I'm here" | Apps unlock. Time at the gym can't be measured, so the day counts when Apple Health confirms 20 min, or when they add a progress photo (like home workouts). *(Change: today "I'm here" counts the day at once)* |
| Gym is right next to home (home is inside the gym area) | Time only counts after the app saw them leave home, or with Apple Health. |
| Two workouts in one day | One sticker, and the day counts once. |

## Flow 4 — Skips and Reschedules

```text
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  3 workouts in a week = STREAK KEPT          │
 │  3 is the minimum. nobody can plan fewer.    │
 │  a home workout counts as a full workout     │
 │  (max 3 home workouts a month)               │
 │  the week runs Monday → Sunday               │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**No skip budget.** Skipping is always allowed — it's the user's life. The **same screen** appears whether they tap "can't today" or slept through the alarm.

**Change:** remove the current 28-day "easy skip" count. When it runs out today, the same 3 buttons just appear in a different order with different words. It changes nothing, and nobody ever sees the number.

```text
 [can't today]   or   missed the alarm (notification when the lock lifts)
   │
   ▼
 Can you still reach 3 this week with the planned days you have left?
   │
   ├─ NO  (goal is 3, or goal is higher but too many days were missed)
   │   │
   │   │  "make it up. this keeps your streak."
   │   │
   │   ├─ ⭐ [reschedule]   ← shown FIRST, recommended
   │   ├─    [home workout, 20 min]
   │   └─    [skip]
   │
   └─ YES (goal is above 3, and there's still room)
       │
       │  "no problem. you need 2 more this week."
       │
       ├─ [skip]            ← shown FIRST, nothing lost
       ├─ [reschedule]      ← still offered
       └─ [home workout, 20 min]

 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

 [reschedule]
   └─ pick when:  [later today]  [Thu]  [Sat]  [Sun]
        │   only days left THIS week that aren't already planned
        │   default time = their usual gym time (can be changed)
        │   no times inside sleep hours
        │
        └─ that day: REAL alarm + apps LOCKED, same flow as a gym day
             ├─ goes ──► workout done ──► +1 · "you came back" notification
             └─ misses it
                  ├─ free days still left this week ──► offer [reschedule] again
                  └─ no days left ──────────────────► counts as a skip

 [home workout, 20 min]   max 3 a month · resets on the 1st
   └─ in-app 20-min timer ──► timer ends ──► apps UNLOCKED
        └─ APPLE HEALTH CHECK (20 min or longer, not typed in by hand)
             ├─ found ─────► +1 day · 🏅 verified sticker
             └─ not found ─► "no workout in Apple Health? add a progress photo to count it."
                               └─ [take photo]  camera only · after the timer · same day
                                    ├─ photo saved to Progress ──► +1 day · 🏅
                                    └─ no photo ──► doesn't count (can add one until midnight)

 [skip]
   └─ apps UNLOCKED · nothing counted · the week check updates
```

- **Change:** remove "reschedule within 24h." Today it promises "tomorrow" but only reminds you on your next planned day (for M/T/W that's next Monday), unlocks your apps, and costs nothing — an unlimited free skip. The "later today" button also just unlocks today.
- **Change:** today tapping "done" on the home-workout timer counts as a full day with no check. There's also a 15-min option and no monthly limit. Keep the full day. Add the Apple Health or photo check and the limit of 3 a month, and remove the 15-min option.

## Flow 5 — Freezes

How one is earned, held and spent.

```text
 EARN  (max 2 per calendar year, Jan 1 → Dec 31)
   ❄️ #1 ──► after 2 months of use    🔔 "you earned a freeze."
   ❄️ #2 ──► after 6 months of use    🔔 "you earned your 2nd freeze."
   "months of use" counts from the day you joined.
   from the next year on, it counts from Jan 1 → freezes arrive Mar 1 and Jul 1.
   unused freezes expire on Dec 31.

 SUNDAY MIDNIGHT: did the week reach 3?
   │
   ├─ YES ──► streak +1
   │
   └─ NO  ──► have a ❄️?
               ├─ YES ──► ❄️ used AUTOMATICALLY · streak safe
               │          (a frozen week doesn't add to the streak or break it)
               └─ NO  ──► streak back to 0
                          Monday message: "new week," not "you lost"

 PLANNING AHEAD (holiday, sick)
   "freeze this week" ──► costs 1 ❄️ now
      ├─ you keep the week anyway ──► ❄️ refunded
      └─ gym alarms PAUSE that week (the night lock KEEPS going)
```

**Change:** today a freeze is earned every 4 kept weeks in a row (max 2 held), and nothing tells the user when they get one.

**Change:** today the gym alarm still rings and locks you on a frozen week.

Using a freeze automatically, planning one ahead, and the refund are already built.

```text
 ❄️ 1   May 10    2 months of use ──► 🔔 "you earned a freeze"

```

## Skip, Reschedule, Home Workout and Freeze Edge Cases

| What happens | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Missed on Sunday | No days left to reschedule to. Freeze or reset. |
| Plans 7 days | No free days to reschedule to, but 4 days of slack. The home workout is still there if any are left this month. |
| No free days left, but the week is still reachable | Hide "reschedule" and say "your planned days left are enough." |
| Misses a rescheduled day | Offered again while free days remain this week. Nothing carries into next week. |
| Wants to cancel a reschedule | Allowed. It turns into a skip. |
| Rescheduled day falls on a rest day with the plain alarm | The rescheduled alarm replaces the plain one. |
| Reschedule time inside sleep hours | Can't be picked. |
| 4th home workout in a month | Greyed out: "3 of 3 home workouts used. back on the 1st." |
| Home-workout photo from the library, or taken before the timer | Doesn't count. It must be a camera photo taken after the timer starts, the same day. |
| Already added a progress photo earlier today | The workout photo replaces it (one photo a day is already built). |
| Two workouts in one day (gym + home, or two gym visits) | Counts as 1 day. Already built. |
| Changes the plan in the middle of a week | The new rule starts next Monday. *(Built (`2026-09-26`): today, raising the plan re-checks past weeks and can reset the streak)* |
| Existing user with only 1–2 gym days | Asked to add a day next time they open the app. The 3-day rule starts next Monday. *(Built (`2026-09-26`))* |
| Tries to drop to 2 gym days | Refused: "3 gym days is the minimum." *(Built (`2026-09-26`))* |
| Target picker in onboarding | 3 to 7. *(Built (`2026-09-26`): today it allows 1, and someone planning 2 days only needs 2 for the streak)* |
| Joined in November | 2 months lands in January. The new year restarts the count, so the first freeze comes Mar 1. |
| Still holding a freeze on Dec 31 | It expires. The new year brings 2 more (Mar 1, Jul 1). |
| Sick for 2 weeks with 1 freeze | Week 1 frozen, week 2 breaks the streak. Already built. |

## Everything to Change

**Sleep and the night lock**

1. The night lock is always on for the sleep schedule's nights. Remove the on/off switch and the custom window. Ask everyone for a bedtime and wake time. Add the background Screen Time extension. A new bedtime starts tomorrow night. Sleep must be at least 5 hours.
2. Gym and sleep never overlap on the dial. Dragging the gym into the night slides the night later, keeping its length, with travel time (≥30 min) in between, plus a short note.
3. A gym visit during sleep hours: the night lock stays on, the visit counts, and a "cost you sleep" notification is sent.
4. Day card: 3 gym days minimum. The night that ends on a gym morning is protected even when bedtime is after midnight.

**Alarms**

5. Choose the flow by the gap between waking and the gym, not by the clock. Fix evening users' wake time.
6. Add the "time to go" alarm for go-later users.
7. An ignored alarm ends when the lock lifts and sends the "pick a day to make it up" notification.
8. "Running late" keeps the lock and sets a real alarm. Options that run into sleep hours are hidden.
9. Optional plain wake alarm on rest days.

**At the gym**

10. The day counts when the workout is done, not on arrival: an Apple Health workout of 20+ min (not typed by hand, started at the gym), or 20 min at the gym. Keep checking Apple Health for 5 hours. Track time at the gym and when they leave.
11. On arrival: "20 minutes and today counts." When the workout is done, a notification at 30 min or on leaving, built from their real numbers, which opens the Progress spotlight.
12. Fix the bug: only alarm notifications may start a session. **(Built: `2026-09-26`)**
13. The "verified" sticker means the workout was done. GPS arrivals alone become "gym visits."
14. "I'm here" (GPS failed): the day counts with Apple Health or a progress photo.

**Skips, reschedules, home workouts**

15. One skip screen (reschedule, home workout, skip) with "make it up. this keeps your streak." Remove "reschedule within 24h" and the 28-day skip count.
16. Real reschedule: later today or a free day this week, with a real alarm and lock, no sleep-hour times. Offered again while free days remain this week.
17. A home workout counts as a full workout, verified by Apple Health (20+ min) or a camera progress photo. Max 3 a month. Remove the 15-min option.

**Streak and freezes**

18. 3 is the minimum everywhere: onboarding picker 3–7, and the streak always needs 3. **(Built: `2026-09-26`)**
19. Save each week's rule when the week starts, so plan changes never rewrite past weeks. **(Built: `2026-09-26`)**
20. Count a visit on the day the alarm rang, and let unplanned days count.
21. Freezes: 2 per calendar year (at 2 and 6 months of use; Mar 1 and Jul 1 in later years). They expire Dec 31, with a notification when one is earned. A planned freeze pauses that week's gym alarms.
22. Streak-at-risk notification plus a banner on Home.

## Risks to Know About

1. **The night lock needs the background extension.** It still doesn't exist in the project. Without it, the lock only kicks in when the user opens the app after bedtime, and the app's wording has to say so honestly.
2. **Each fallback proves less.** Apple Health proves effort, but needs a watch or a workout app. 20 minutes at the gym proves presence, not effort. A photo proves the least. The limit of 3 home workouts a month caps how far the photo route can be gamed.
3. **Apple hides whether Health read access was denied.** A denied permission looks exactly like "no workout found." Every "not found" message must point to the permission and offer the fallback, and never accuse the user of not training.
4. **iOS can report leaving the gym a few minutes late.** The "when they leave" notification and the 20-minute clock can be off by a few minutes, so the wording shouldn't promise exact timing.

## Confirmed Decisions

These were open questions while the flow was being written. They are final.

- **Wake & Go vs Go Later is decided by a 2-hour gap** between wake time and gym time (Step 0).
- **The day counts when the workout is done, not on arrival.**
- **In later years, freezes arrive Mar 1 and Jul 1, and unused ones expire Dec 31.**
- **The night lock stays on during a late-night gym visit.**

## Appendix B — Words Used in This File

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Gym day | A weekday on the gym days card. The gym alarm rings and the gym lock runs on these days. |
| Sleep night | A night the sleep schedule runs, so the night lock runs. A night belongs to the day it starts: "Sun" means Sunday 23:00 to Monday 06:30. |
| Required night | The night before a gym day. It cannot be turned off. |
| Wake & Go / Go Later | Which flow a user gets, decided in Step 0. |
| Night lock | Distracting apps locked from bedtime to wake time on sleep nights. |
| Gym lock | Distracting apps locked from the gym alarm until the user arrives at the gym, or until the lock lifts. |
| Snooze | The one delay after the first alarm ring, before the second ring. Length is editable by the user (1–15 min), default 5 minutes. Diagrams in this file show the 5-minute default. |
| Session | One run from a gym alarm to its result. |
| Workout done | What counts the day. Defined in Flow 3. |
| Week | Monday to Sunday. |
| Week kept / streak kept | The week reached 3 workouts. |
| Streak | Kept weeks in a row. A frozen week neither adds to it nor breaks it. |
| Freeze | Protects one week that didn't reach 3. See Flow 5. |
| Reschedule | Moving a missed workout to later today or a free day this week, with a real alarm and lock. See Flow 4. |
