Summit volcano, erupting

The central mountain is now one painted volcano: a broad stratovolcano cone with concave flanks, a broken rim, a dark rock top and dry-brush gullies, set into layered ridges and a dark tree line. The lava is computed from the cone itself: every river follows a gully down the slope and is clipped to the cone, so it can never leave the mountain. Quiet and rumbling until the fifth tap on the climb, then it erupts and keeps going.
